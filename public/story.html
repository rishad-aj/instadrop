FAQ

Instagram Story Downloader FAQ

Everything you should know about downloading Instagram stories.

Will the account owner see that I downloaded their story?

No. Because you never log in to Instagram on Instadrop, your account is never associated with the request.

Why do some active 24-hour stories not load?

Instagram often restricts active 24-hour stories to logged-in sessions. However, once a story is saved to a profile's public Story Highlights, Instadrop can download every slide without login.

Can I also extract MP3 audio from a video story?

Yes — every video story result includes an Audio (MP3) button to save its soundtrack as an MP3 file.
