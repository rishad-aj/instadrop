Privacy Policy

Last updated 14 September 2026

17 sections

1Summary in Plain English

Instadrop is a browser-based Instagram media downloader. It is designed so that the app you are reading this in never needs to collect, store, sell or share your personal data.

  • We have no accounts, no sign-up and no login.
  • We do not store the links you paste.
  • We do not store the files you download.
  • We do not run advertising, tracking pixels or third-party analytics.
  • Your settings and download history live only in your own browser.
In short: the media you ask for is resolved from Instagram's public endpoints and handed straight to your device. We never see it and have no copy of it.

2Who We Are

This Privacy Policy explains how Instadrop ("we", "us", "our") handles information in connection with the Instadrop website, installable web app and related service (the "Service").

For the purposes of the UK GDPR, the EU GDPR and similar laws, Instadrop is the data controller in respect of the limited processing described below. You can contact us at legal@instadrop.web.app.

This Policy should be read together with our Terms and Conditions. It does not apply to Instagram or to any other third-party service you may use alongside Instadrop.

3Information We Do Not Collect

We want to be unusually clear about what Instadrop does not do, because most downloader sites do the opposite:

  • No account data. There is no registration, no email address, no password and no profile.
  • No Instagram credentials. We never ask for, receive or transmit your Instagram username or password, and we never log you in to Instagram on your behalf.
  • No copy of your links. The Instagram URL you paste is used in your browser to resolve media and is not stored on our systems.
  • No copy of your media. Downloaded files go from Instagram (or the resolver in your browser) to your device. They never pass through a storage server that we control.
  • No advertising identifiers, no tracking pixels, no cross-site tracking and no behavioural profiling.
  • No third-party analytics SDKs, session recorders or heat-mapping tools.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of personal data with data brokers or advertisers, because we do not hold personal data to sell.

4Information Processed in Your Browser

Almost everything that could be described as "processing" happens locally on your own device, inside your browser, and never reaches us:

  • The Instagram link you paste is held in memory so that the app can resolve it, and is discarded when you close the page.
  • Media files are fetched directly by your browser and saved by your browser's normal download mechanism.
  • Audio-to-MP3 conversion is performed on your device using in-browser WebAssembly software; the audio is not uploaded anywhere.
  • Your theme choice and any download history are kept in your browser's local storage so the app remembers your preferences between visits.

Because this processing is local, we cannot see it, access it, recover it or disclose it. Clearing your browser's site data removes all of it permanently.

5Local Storage and How to Clear It

Instadrop uses your browser's local storage (and, where available, a service-worker cache) to make the app work offline and to remember your preferences. The keys currently used are:

  • instadrop_theme - stores your light, dark or system theme choice.
  • instadrop_history - stores a short list of recent links, so you can re-use them quickly. It stays on your device and can be cleared from within the app.

You can delete all of this at any time by clearing site data or cookies for this site in your browser settings, or by using the clear control shown next to your history in the app. Clearing it has no effect on us, because we hold no matching copy.

We do not use advertising cookies, and no cookie is set for the purpose of tracking you across other websites.

6Server Logs and Technical Data

The Service is served as a static website by a hosting provider. Like virtually all web hosts, that provider automatically records basic technical information when a page or asset is requested.

Those logs may include your IP address, the date and time of the request, the page or asset requested, your browser's user-agent string, and a referring URL if your browser sends one.

This information is generated by the hosting infrastructure rather than by Instadrop, is used solely to serve the site, to keep it secure, to diagnose faults and to prevent abuse, and is retained only for the short period set by the hosting provider's standard log-retention policy.

We do not use these logs to build profiles of individual visitors, we do not combine them with any other data, and we do not use them for advertising.

7Third-Party Services

To function, the Service loads a small number of third-party components. When your browser requests those resources, the relevant provider may receive your IP address and standard request headers, as is inherent in any request made over the web:

  • Instagram (Meta Platforms, Inc.) - the app requests publicly available Instagram endpoints in order to resolve the media you asked for. Instagram's own privacy policy governs that interaction.
  • Content delivery networks - used to load a UI component stylesheet and a WebAssembly media-conversion library.
  • Google Fonts - used to load the Inter typeface.
  • The hosting provider - serves the static files that make up the app.

None of these providers receives the Instagram link you paste or the file you download from Instadrop itself. We do not control third-party services and we encourage you to read their privacy policies.

8Legal Bases for Processing

Where the UK or EU GDPR applies, we rely on the following legal bases for the very limited processing that is attributable to us:

  • Legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)) - serving the website, keeping it secure and preventing abuse through standard server logs, which is necessary for the operation of the Service and is balanced against your rights.
  • Consent (Article 6(1)(a)) - where you affirmatively choose to use a feature that requires a request to a third party, such as resolving an Instagram link.
  • Legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c)) - where we are required to retain or disclose information in order to comply with the law.

We do not process your personal data for the purpose of automated decision-making or profiling.

9Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have some or all of the following rights in relation to your personal data: access, correction, erasure, restriction of processing, objection to processing, data portability, withdrawal of consent, and the right not to be subject to solely automated decisions.

Because Instadrop is designed not to hold personal data, we may in practice hold nothing to which these rights can attach. If you nonetheless wish to exercise a right, contact us at legal@instadrop.web.app and we will respond within the period required by law.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data-protection supervisory authority. If you are in the UK, that is the Information Commissioner's Office.

10California and Other US State Privacy Rights

If you are a California resident, the CCPA as amended by the CPRA gives you rights to know, to delete, to correct, to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information, to limit the use of sensitive personal information, and not to be discriminated against for exercising those rights.

Instadrop does not sell personal information, does not share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising, does not collect sensitive personal information for the purpose of inferring characteristics, and does not knowingly collect information from consumers under 16.

Similar rights are available under the privacy laws of other US states, including Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah and Texas. Requests may be sent to legal@instadrop.web.app and we will honour them to the extent they apply to data we actually hold.

11International Data Transfers

The Instagram endpoints, content delivery networks and font services used by the Service may be located outside your country, including in the United States.

Any such transfer is made by the third-party provider concerned under its own safeguards (such as standard contractual clauses) rather than by Instadrop, and is inherent in your browser requesting those resources directly.

Where Instadrop itself ever transfers personal data internationally, it will do so only with appropriate safeguards in place.

12Security

The Service is delivered over HTTPS and is built to minimise risk by not collecting data in the first place.

Because no account, no credential and no content is stored on our systems, there is no central database of user data that could be breached, leaked or sold.

You are responsible for the security of your own device, browser and downloads. Only download from sources you trust, and keep your browser and operating system up to date.

13Data Retention

We retain no personal data, because we collect none. There is nothing to retain and nothing to delete.

Data held in your browser (theme preference and download history) is retained until you clear it or your browser evicts it.

Technical server logs generated by our hosting provider are retained for the short period defined by that provider's standard policy, after which they are deleted or anonymised.

14Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under the minimum age of digital consent in your jurisdiction).

Because we do not collect personal information at all, a child's use of the Service does not result in any data being sent to us; however, parents and guardians should supervise minors' use of third-party platforms such as Instagram.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to a third party through the Service, please contact that third party, and contact us at legal@instadrop.web.app if you have any concern about Instadrop itself.

15Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

We do not track you across websites, so there is nothing for a Do Not Track (DNT) or Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal to switch off.

We honour DNT and GPC signals in the sense that the Service behaves identically whether or not such a signal is present: no tracking occurs either way.

16Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The current version is always the one published on this page, and the effective date and version number are shown at the top.

Material changes will be reflected by a new effective date. Continued use of the Service after an update means you accept the updated Policy.
