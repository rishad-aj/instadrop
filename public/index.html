FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you might want to know before your first download.

Is Instadrop really free?

Yes — completely. There are no accounts, subscriptions, paywalls or download limits, and there are no ads anywhere on the site. Everything runs in your browser for free.

Are there ads or trackers?

None at all. Instadrop is 100% ad-free — no banner ads, no popups, no redirects and no tracking scripts. Your links and activity are never collected or sold.

Do I need to log in to Instagram?

No. Instadrop works with public content only and never asks for your credentials. Public reels, posts, profile pictures and highlights work without any login.

Why is the profile picture a small size?

Instagram serves profile photos in a small size to anonymous visitors. You always get the original file exactly as Instagram provides it — no re-encoding, no fake upscaling.

Can I download live stories?

Usually not — active stories expire after 24 hours and Instagram only shows them to logged-in accounts. Public story highlights, however, work fine here.

Where are my downloads stored?

On your own device. Media streams directly to your browser and is saved where your browser puts downloads — Instadrop doesn't store anything on any server.

Is downloading Instagram media legal?

Download only content you have the right to — your own posts, or content the owner has shared for downloading. Respect creators' rights and the platform's terms of service.
